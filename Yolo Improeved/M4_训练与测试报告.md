# M4 实验报告｜YOLO11s + P2 + DySample

> 历史报告：以下为原仓库 2026-11-10、RTX 5090 D 的记录。2026-11-12–13 的 RTX 3090 配对复现实验独立保存，最新结果见 [仓库实验报告](../EXPERIMENT_REPORT.md)。不同硬件与运行的数字不得混用。

实验日期为 2026 年 11 月 10 日。M4 完成 100 轮训练，按验证集 AP50 选中第 78 轮权重。该权重在 240 张独立测试图像上取得 **AP50 87.30%、AP50:95 48.12%、F1 83.77%**。本机 RTX 5090 D 上的批量推理吞吐为 **29.42 张/秒**。

## 模型与训练设置

保留 YOLO11s 主干及 P3/P4/P5 路径。从 P3 经 DySample 上采样两倍，与主干 P2 特征拼接，再用 C3k2 融合。检测头使用 P2、P3、P4 和 P5 四个尺度，对应步长 4、8、16 和 32。使用官方 `yolo11s.pt` 初始化兼容参数，新增层保留自身初始化，输出单类 `bird`。

沿用 [Beach Bird Detection Dataset v1.0](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/liugalhigh/beach-bird) 的原始划分。图像可读性、标签边界、类别及跨划分完全重复文件检查均通过。

| 划分 | 图像数 | 标注框数 |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| 训练 | 1,369 | 8,663 |
| 验证 | 291 | 1,843 |
| 测试 | 240 | 1,518 |

训练使用 640 × 640 输入、FP16 AMP、随机种子 42、批大小 8、梯度累积 2 步。AdamW 学习率从 0.0001 预热至 0.001，随后余弦降至 0.00001。权重衰减为 0.0005，偏置和归一化参数除外。第 91 轮关闭 Mosaic。每轮完整验证一次，以 AP50 为主、AP50:95 为次选出最佳权重，同分时保留较早轮次。

## 训练与验证结果

训练于悉尼时间 16:10 开始，17:24 完成，共 8,600 次优化器更新。训练循环累计 **26 分 42 秒**，验证累计 **43 分 35 秒**，含准备、保存等开销的总历时约 **1 小时 13 分 40 秒**。训练阶段峰值分配显存为 **3.86 GiB**。

| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 1 | 57.35% | 25.28% | 56.65% |
| 10 | 65.82% | 32.38% | 64.71% |
| 25 | 77.71% | 39.58% | 75.56% |
| 50 | 82.90% | 43.90% | 78.66% |
| **78（最佳）** | **88.04%** | **46.82%** | **84.22%** |
| 100 | 87.39% | 46.78% | 84.32% |

# M4 Experiment Report | YOLO11s + P2 + DySample

Experiment date: 10 November 2026. M4 completed 100 training epochs. Validation AP50 selected the epoch 78 checkpoint. On 240 held-out test images, it achieved **87.30% AP50, 48.12% AP50:95 and 83.77% F1**. Batch inference throughput on the local RTX 5090 D was **29.42 images/s**.

## Model and training setup

The model retains the YOLO11s backbone and P3/P4/P5 paths. DySample upsamples P3 by a factor of two. The upsampled features are concatenated with the backbone P2 features and fused with C3k2. The detection head uses P2, P3, P4 and P5 with strides of 4, 8, 16 and 32. Compatible parameters were initialized from the official `yolo11s.pt` weights. New layers retained their own initialization. The model detects one class, `bird`.

The experiment used the original splits of the [Beach Bird Detection Dataset v1.0](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/liugalhigh/beach-bird). The audit checked image readability, label bounds, class IDs and exact duplicates across splits.

| Split | Images | Ground-truth boxes |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Train | 1,369 | 8,663 |
| Validation | 291 | 1,843 |
| Test | 240 | 1,518 |

Training used 640 × 640 inputs, FP16 automatic mixed precision, seed 42, batch size 8 and two gradient accumulation steps. The AdamW learning rate warmed up from 0.0001 to 0.001, then decayed to 0.00001 on a cosine schedule. Weight decay was 0.0005, excluding biases and normalization parameters. Mosaic was disabled starting at epoch 91. The full validation set was evaluated every epoch. Checkpoint selection ranked by AP50, then AP50:95, then earlier epoch for exact ties.

## Training and validation results

Training started at 16:10 and finished at 17:24 Sydney time, completing 8,600 optimizer updates. Training loops took **26 min 42 s**, validation took **43 min 35 s**, and total elapsed time, including preparation and checkpoint writes, was approximately **1 h 13 min 40 s**. Peak allocated GPU memory during training was **3.86 GiB**.

| Epoch | Validation AP50 | Validation AP50:95 | Validation F1 |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 1 | 57.35% | 25.28% | 56.65% |
| 10 | 65.82% | 32.38% | 64.71% |
| 25 | 77.71% | 39.58% | 75.56% |
| 50 | 82.90% | 43.90% | 78.66% |
| **78 (selected)** | **88.04%** | **46.82%** | **84.22%** |
| 100 | 87.39% | 46.78% | 84.32% |

## Held-out test results

After training, the epoch 78 `best.pt` checkpoint produced detections in original image coordinates. AP evaluation used a score prefilter of 0.001, NMS IoU of 0.60 and a cap of 300 detections per image. Precision, recall, F1 and false-positive counts used a confidence threshold of 0.25 and one-to-one matching at IoU 0.50.

| Metric | Validation (epoch 78) | Test (same checkpoint) |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| AP50 | 88.04% | **87.30%** |
| AP50:95 | 46.82% | **48.12%** |
| AP75 | 43.33% | 47.11% |
| AP-small | 31.96% | 31.67% |
| AR-small | 50.54% | 46.00% |
| Precision | 83.06% | 83.04% |
| Recall | 85.40% | 84.52% |
| F1 | 84.22% | **83.77%** |
| Mean false positives per negative image | 1.03 | 0.37 |

At confidence 0.25, the test set yielded **1,283 true positives, 262 false positives and 235 false negatives**. The 27 images without birds produced 10 false positives. The export used for AP evaluation contains 13,435 detections across all 240 test images.

| Test density (birds/image) | Images | Ground-truth boxes | AP50 | AP50:95 | AP-small | AR-small |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Negative (0) | 27 | 0 | — | — | — | — |
| Sparse (1–10) | 170 | 588 | 90.43% | 58.30% | 33.54% | 46.09% |
| Medium (11–50) | 41 | 808 | 86.57% | 42.13% | 32.23% | 45.34% |
| Dense (51+) | 2 | 122 | 78.98% | 31.70% | 29.90% | 48.42% |

Test AP fell from 87.30% at IoU 0.50 to 47.11% at IoU 0.75. AP50:95 was higher on sparse images than on medium or dense images. The dense group contains just two images, so individual images can have a large effect on its scores.

## Local inference benchmark

The benchmark ran on Windows with an NVIDIA GeForce RTX 5090 D, PyTorch 2.12.0+cu130 and Ultralytics 8.3.221, using FP16 automatic mixed precision and batch size 8. Images were preloaded into CPU memory. After 20 warmup batches, three timed passes processed all 240 images in 30 batches per pass. Timing included preprocessing, device transfer, the forward pass, NMS and restoration to original image coordinates.

| Metric | Measured |
| --- | ---: |
| Full test-set time, three passes | 8.19, 8.18, 8.11 s |
| Throughput | 29.42 images/s |
| Amortized time per image | 33.99 ms |
| Mean batch time | 271.91 ms |
| P95 batch time | 545.62 ms |
| Peak allocated GPU memory during inference | 0.53 GiB |

Training and benchmarking used the local RTX 5090 D. The project protocol specifies a Colab T4 benchmark, which remains pending. CUDA backpropagation through DySample's `grid_sample` can be nondeterministic. This report covers one M4 run. Matched baseline YOLO and P2-only ablation results are needed to quantify the improvement and the contribution of each module.
